# Research disclaimer

This repository is a quantitative research project, not a trading system or
investment advisory service.

- Forecasts concern next-day volatility, not index direction.
- Volatility forecast errors are not trading returns.
- Public data may contain errors, revisions, or coverage limitations.
- Backtests do not model transaction costs, liquidity, execution, taxes, or
  personal constraints.
- High-risk alerts are research signals and must not be treated as
  recommendations.

Nothing in this repository constitutes financial, legal, tax, or investment
advice.
